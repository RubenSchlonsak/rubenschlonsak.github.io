The web fonts in this folder are self-hosted copies of three open-source
typefaces, each released under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1
(https://openfontlicense.org):

  Instrument Serif   https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Instrument+Serif
  Schibsted Grotesk  https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Schibsted+Grotesk
  JetBrains Mono     https://fonts.google.com/specimen/JetBrains+Mono

The files are the unmodified latin and latin-ext WOFF2 subsets as served by
Google Fonts. Copyright remains with the respective font authors; see the
